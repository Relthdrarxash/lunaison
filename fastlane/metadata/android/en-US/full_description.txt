Lunaison tracks your menstrual cycle without knowing anything about you: no account, no ads, no trackers, not even Internet access. Everything stays on your phone.

LOG IN A FEW SECONDS
Flow, pain, symptoms, mood and a note if you like. Everything is optional, and a forgotten day can be filled in later. You can also turn on optional ovulation signs (cervical mucus and basal body temperature) or symptoms suited to irregular cycles and perimenopause.

HONEST, ADAPTABLE FORECASTS
Lunaison estimates your next period from your recent cycles and shows a likely range of dates. You can display an estimated fertile window, enable irregular cycles or perimenopause mode, or pause predictions altogether (pregnancy, postpartum, continuous contraception). If your period is much later than usual, a calm, non-alarming card gives practical context.

UNDERSTAND EACH PHASE AND BREATHE
What is happening in your body across all 4 cycle phases, what helps, what tends to come back for you based on your previous cycles, plus a 1-minute guided breathing exercise (4s in / 6s out) for cramps or tension.

REFERENCED GUIDES AND A CONSULTATION SUMMARY
Your cycle, pain, food, premenstrual syndrome, when to see a doctor, toxic shock: each guide cites the studies it relies on. You can also copy or share a structured 3- or 6-month health summary for your doctor, gynecologist or midwife.

DISCREET REMINDERS AND PIN LOCK
Reminders before your period, to log your days, or daily for your contraception or medication. On the lock screen, only "Lunaison" is shown. You can lock the app with a 4-digit PIN and choose from 4 soft WCAG AA color themes. A discreet home screen widget is also included.

SHARE WITH YOUR PARTNER WITHOUT ANY SERVER
For people aged 18 and over: share a summary via your messaging app (SMS, Signal, WhatsApp...) or via an offline QR code generated and scanned right inside Lunaison, complete with phase-by-phase partner support tips. Nothing ever goes through a server.

YOUR DATA BELONGS TO YOU
Spreadsheet (CSV) export and import, Android encrypted device-to-device backup, and complete erasure in one tap. Lunaison is free and open source (GPL-3.0): its code is public.

Lunaison is neither a medical device nor a method of contraception.
