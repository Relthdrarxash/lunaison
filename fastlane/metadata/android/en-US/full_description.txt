Lunaison tracks your menstrual cycle without knowing anything about you: no account, no ads, no trackers, not even Internet access. Everything stays on your phone.

LOG IN A FEW SECONDS
Flow, pain, symptoms, mood and a note if you like. Everything is optional, and a forgotten day can be filled in later.

HONEST FORECASTS
Lunaison estimates your next period from your recent cycles and shows a likely range of dates, which narrows as you log more cycles.

UNDERSTAND EACH PHASE
What is happening in your body, what helps, and what tends to come back for you during this phase based on your previous cycles.

REFERENCED GUIDES
Your cycle, pain, food, premenstrual syndrome, when to see a doctor, toxic shock: each guide cites the studies it relies on.

KNOW WHEN TO SEE A DOCTOR
Repeated severe pain, periods longer than 8 days, cycles outside the usual range, bleeding between periods: Lunaison points it out, without ever making a diagnosis.

DISCREET REMINDERS
Before your period and to log your days. On the lock screen, only the app name is shown.

LET YOUR PARTNER KNOW
For people aged 18 and over: a message ready to send by SMS, WhatsApp or Signal, which you review and send yourself. Nothing is sent automatically.

YOUR DATA BELONGS TO YOU
Spreadsheet export and complete erasure in one tap. Lunaison is free and open source (GPL-3.0): its code is public.

Lunaison is neither a medical device nor a method of contraception.
