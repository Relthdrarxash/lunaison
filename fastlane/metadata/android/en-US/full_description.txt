Lunaison is an intimate menstrual cycle and symptom tracker designed for everyone experiencing cycles, from teenagers to adults and peri-menopause.

Fully open-source (MPL-2.0), free, ad-free, account-free, and 100% offline: Lunaison requires zero network permissions in its Android manifest. Your private reproductive health data is stored strictly on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.

CYCLE TRACKING AND PHYSIOLOGY
• Menstrual calendar with cycle phases: menstruation, follicular phase, estimated ovulation, luteal phase.
• Daily symptom logging: flow intensity, pain, cervical mucus, mood, energy, sleep, digestion.
• Interactive reproductive anatomy diagram: explore uterine and ovarian structures and use the cycle slider (D1 to D28) to observe endometrial thickening, follicular growth, ovulation, and corpus luteum regression.
• Symptom correlation engine: objective pattern detection connecting cycle phases with recurring symptoms.

EVIDENCE-BASED EDUCATION
• Concise, referenced articles (ACOG, ESRH, Inserm) explaining physiology without condescending metaphors.
• Adolescent cycle variability guidelines (21 to 45 days according to ACOG 2015).
• Comprehensive guide on endometriosis, chronic pelvic pain, and red flag symptoms.

STRICT PRIVACY
• Zero data collection, zero analytics, zero trackers.
• Local export and encrypted backup.
• 100% functional without an Internet connection.
