Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026. The French version is the reference text.

In short: Lunaison collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics.

What the app stores

On your phone, and only on your phone:

  • the days you log: flow, pain, symptoms, moods and personal notes;
  • your answers at the start: age group, approximate date of your last period, usual cycle length;
  • your reminder settings;
  • if you use sharing, the first name, number and messaging app of the person you chose.

This information concerns your health and your intimate life. That is why Lunaison never sends it off your phone.

What we receive

Nothing. Lunaison does not have permission to access the Internet: it technically cannot send anything. Nobody, not even the app's author, has access to your data.

No cloud backup

Lunaison excludes its data from Android backup and from transfer to a new phone. To keep it, use the spreadsheet export in the settings: you choose where to save the file.

Switching phones or apps

When switching phones or migrating away from proprietary trackers, Lunaison lets you directly import your historical data (including password-protected ZIP archives from the Period Calendar app or CSV files). Decryption and parsing happen strictly in your device's memory, without sending any byte to the Internet.

Sharing with a partner

Lunaison offers two serverless sharing modes under your sole control:

  • sending a message that you review and send yourself through the messaging app you chose (SMS, WhatsApp, Signal or another app);
  • generating a QR Code (or a short code shareable via SMS or messaging) containing only the dates of your last six periods and, if you opt in, the estimated fertile window. Your personal notes are never included. Camera permission is only requested if you open the built-in QR Code scanner.

To choose a contact, Lunaison uses the system picker, which only gives it access to the chosen contact. Lunaison does not ask for access to your address book.

Notifications

Reminders are calculated and scheduled on your phone. On the lock screen, they only show the app's name.

Erasing your data

Lunaison's settings let you erase everything in one tap. Uninstalling the app also erases all its data.

The Lunaison website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. Like any host, GitHub may keep technical connection logs (IP address, date, time, page requested).

Your rights

Since no personal data is collected by Lunaison's author, there is no data on our side to access, correct or delete. For any question: pilmil.studio@proton.me.

Open code

Lunaison's code is public and open source: anyone can check all of the above by reading it on GitHub.

Lunaison is neither a medical device nor a method of contraception. It does not replace the advice of a health professional.
