The short answers

What people ask us most often. A question missing? Write to pilmil.studio@proton.me.

Health and forecasts

Can I use Lunaison as contraception?

No. The ovulation dates shown are estimates that can be off by several days. Even the most rigorous cycle-awareness methods often fail in typical use. A doctor, a midwife or a sexual health clinic can help you choose a method of contraception.

Why is the forecast a range and not a precise day?

Because nobody has perfectly identical cycles. The range reflects how much your own cycles vary: the more regular they are, the narrower it gets. The details are on the How it works page.

My period is late, what should I do?

A delay of a few days is common. Stress, travel, illness, a change in weight or activity, or a pregnancy can explain it. If you are unsure about pregnancy, a pharmacy test gives a quick answer. Three months without a period warrants seeing a doctor.

Lunaison shows me a "worth mentioning" alert. Is it serious?

It is not a diagnosis. It means your notes differ from a medical reference range (repeated severe pain, very long periods, cycles outside the usual lengths…) and that it is worth mentioning at your next appointment. The linked guide explains why.

My cycles are irregular, is the app still useful?

Yes. The forecast will be wider, but the history, the advice for each phase and the alerts remain useful, and the history is valuable for a health professional.

I am under 18, can I use it?

Yes, from age 13. The cycle reference ranges are then adapted to the first years of periods, which are more irregular. Sharing with a partner is turned off.

Privacy

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Lunaison does not have permission to access the Internet, and its data is excluded from cloud backup. Nobody, not even the app's author, has access to it. See the privacy policy.

How does sharing with my partner work?

You have two serverless options: prepare a short message ("My period has started", "I'm in pain today", "Explain my current phase"…) that you send yourself through your messaging app, or share your cycle estimates via an in-app QR Code or Android's share sheet (SMS, Signal, WhatsApp…). Your partner can then view your current phase, next period range and concrete support tips on their phone, without ever receiving your personal notes.

What if I change phones?

Export your data from the settings: you get a spreadsheet file that you keep wherever you like. Importing it into Lunaison on the new phone is one of the features being considered.

Can I import my history from Clue or Flo?

Not yet. It is being considered, but these apps' export formats are not publicly documented. If you are willing to share an anonymized export to help us build it, write to us.

How do I erase everything?

Settings, then "Erase everything". Uninstalling the app also erases all its data.

The app

Why is it free? What's the catch?

There is none. Lunaison is a personal, open source project, with no ads, no subscription and no selling of data. The code is public on GitHub: anyone can check what the app does.

Will there be an iPhone version?

Not for now: Lunaison is developed for Android only. Sharing by message, however, works whatever phone your partner has.

How do I install it?

Lunaison will arrive on Google Play after its testing phase. In the meantime, the signed APK is available in the releases on GitHub. Android 8 or later.

Who is behind Lunaison?

Pilmil Studio, which also publishes Respire (against endless scrolling) and Sommelle (for insomnia). The same principles for all three: free, open source, no account or trackers, based on published studies.

Lunaison is neither a medical device nor a method of contraception. It does not replace the advice of a health professional.
