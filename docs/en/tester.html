Become a tester

Before it can be published on Google Play, Lunaison has to go through a closed test: Google requires at least 12 people to install it and keep it for 14 days in a row. Your help really matters.

1. Send an email

To: pilmil.studio@proton.me

In the email, include:

  • Your Google account address, the one you are signed in with in the Play Store on your phone. Often a Gmail address, but not always: it is the one shown in the Play Store when you tap your profile picture at the top right. If the address does not match, Google will not let you install the test version.
  • Optional: your phone model and Android version. It helps find device-specific bugs.

Write the email

2. What happens next

  1. I add your address to the testers list in the Google Play console.
  2. I reply with a link. Open it on your phone, accept to become a tester, then install Lunaison from the Play Store like any other app.
  3. Keep the app installed for at least 14 days. You do not have to use it every day, but uninstalling it earlier resets Google's counter.

3. Good to know

  • You need an Android phone (version 8 or later) with the Play Store. There is no iPhone version yet.
  • Your address is only used to add you to the test. It is not shared or used for anything else, and I remove it from the list after the test if you ask.
  • Your cycle data never reaches me. The test version is the same as the final one: no account, no tracker, no Internet access.
  • If you installed the APK from GitHub, uninstall it first (export your data from the settings if needed): the two versions are not signed with the same key.
  • Your feedback, even short, is valuable: a bug, an unclear sentence, something missing. Just reply to the email, or open an issue on GitHub.

Lunaison is neither a medical device nor a method of contraception. It does not replace the advice of a health professional.
